General Information

In this page I will provide various links to older game projects I coded. Most of these projects were made during my time at Feevale were I focused on being a game programmer and designer.

Nowadays, I changed my focus to areas of development outside of videogames, however, these projects helped me to evolve my skills and learn how to cooperate with other people during projects and learn several ways to integrate their content into the final product.

Here is a link to my personal Github page that contains some old unity projects from many years ago with all assets and code I did at the time. Although my skills at the time were much more limited, it might be interesting to evaluate my growth over the years.

Git Projects Information

  • Knife Hit Clone: A short test I made for a game dev studio I worked to for a short period. It involved recreating the gameplay of a Mobile game called Knife Hit where the player touches the screen at the right time to hit the targets and gain points.
  • teste-yaw: A short test I made for another game dev studio. It involved recreating the gameplay of any game I liked so i picked a mini game of a RPG I like at the time which was a simple card game.
  • desafio-foguete: Yet another test I made, for a company. It involved creating a very simple scene with a rocket that is launched and you can control where it falls. The most important aspect was the physics because of how the company worked with virtual simulations and training.
  • LudumDare44: A game made in three days for the Ludum Dare game jam. It's the Goblin Launcher game listed on my itch.io page below where you control a knight and must grab and throw goblins out of your castle.
  • Vacuum-Multiplayer: A game I made as the fifth major project during my days at Feevale. It's the multiplayer game ClutchBall listed on my itch.io page below. It's a multiplayer game which was very challenging to make as the multiplayer aspect meant that several instances of the game had to run and execute code which can get out of hand quite quickly if you're not careful.
  • ProjectVII: The final project, called Wu, I made during my time at Feevale.
    It's a 3D hack n' slash game based on ancient chinese mythology. More details are described below on the itch.io section.
  • tile-rpg: A small prototype for a turn based RPG idea I had. You place your units on the grid and select attacks for each of them which they will all execute in a specific order.
  • buster-bros-clone: A small prototype where I tried to recreate the gameplay of Buster Bros, an old 2D Super Nintendo game I like.

This is a link to my itch.io page containing pages with games coded by me. All games can be played directly through the browser or downloaded and played locally. Unfortunately I lost the project files for many of these projects. If I find any, I will create repositories of them in my github page.

itch.io Projects Information

  • Blunderbuster: The first game I made during Feevale. This short platformer was made in one week with the help of two other artists.
    You are a pirate that fires a blunderbuss that damages enemies and also launches you back due to the recoil force which can even allow you to fly by firing downwards. It has one short level and a boss.
  • Helloween Strike: A beat 'em up made in three weeks by me and three other artists.
    I had to struggle a bit with layering due to the fact that the game is entirely 2D but the hitboxes of the attacks need to hit enemies only in front of the player character.
    I had to also create a simple state machine for the enemies and especially the boss so that they can change behaviors to make the fights a bit more interesting. The boss at the end has a specific pattern that cycles between their three attacks.
    The game also has a good and bad ending depending if you killed the innocent NPCs or not.
  • Dynastes: Another game made in one week with the help of three artists.
    You control a hercules beetle with a sword, your mission is to defeat all enemies by using your time stop ability and the slash all enemies before time goes back to normal which kills all of them for good and allows you to go to the next room.
  • ClutchBall: A major college project which had the requirement of being a 3D multiplayer game.
    I had to use a library called Unity Photon that allows multiplayer connections and handles online lobbies and similar features. The coding itself still had to be done by me, which meant knowing when to run certain gameplay logic on the server or client side. Due to limitations, the server has to be one of the players that run the application and creates the lobby for other players.
    This was the most challenging project to me as both the 3D and online multiplayer aspects were new to me at the time.
    To play the game you need a player to create a lobby and have other players run the game and connect to that same lobby.
  • Wu: The last Feevale project I made with the help of other 4 artists.
    It's a hack n' slash demo level where you control a swordsmand character and can execute several elemental combos to defeat enemies. Each special attacks has one of the five elements of ancient chinese culture and if done in a particular order can grant buffs to the player character or debuffs to the enemies.
    The most challenging aspect was making the gameplay and animations feel good and execute the necessary logic at the right times as the hitboxes appear during specific frames in order to make sense and feel good.
    It was during this project I realized that my coding needed to improve as most of the logic I made had several structural problems that made execution order difficult and maintaining and expanding the features difficult. I'm thankful for this time as it made me pursue evolution through books and practice as the years went by.
  • Goblin Launcher: A three days project I made on a game jam with 4 other artists. A 3D game with "2D" gameplay described on the github entry that has the project and code available.
  • Quentuxo ao Resgate: A three weeks project I made with the help of two artists. A 3D game of a scene that takes place during a fire inside a school. You control a firefighter that has to extinguish all flames while teaching the player about different types of fire extinguishers and techniques to apply during a fire.
